FIELD MANUAL

How to Play

Move

WASD / arrow keys, or drag anywhere on touch.

Fight

Kaito and Mira strike nearby. Ren hurls embers, Sora lashes with silk, and Yuki and Aster attack from range. Positioning matters.

Techniques

Space dash. 1–4 equipped techniques. Upgrade them in the Arsenal.

Execution

Every hero has a signature execution. It starts empty; charge it by hitting and killing enemies, then press F or tap its slot at 100% for a cinematic finisher that destroys normal enemies and badly wounds bosses.

Hero Progress

Each shinobi keeps their own level, XP, coins, attributes, skills, and chapter clears. Switching heroes switches saves.

Upgrades

After each wave, choose one attribute upgrade. Costs rise with rank and effects are modest, so every pick is a real choice.

Rage

Hits fill the rage column. Press R when full to transform, then press R again for your Rage Art.

Grow

Kills give XP and coins. Spend coins in the Night Market and skill points in the Arsenal.

Survive

Clear four waves and defeat the chapter boss. Health orbs on the floor glow brighter when you need them.
